Design notes on the Warm direction

What I noticed laying out real screens with the foundations. Turn on Show proposed colour tweaks at the top to compare notes 1 and 2 on the mockups.

1. White bubbles barely separate from the cream background

White on #FFF7EF is about 1.05:1, so in a long Chat other people's bubbles read as floating text. Tweak: give the Message list its own slightly deeper ground (#F6EBDF). Muted text on it is still 4.9:1.

2. Own bubbles in dark mode are very loud

The dark accent #FF8A5C with dark text works for buttons, but a screen full of your own Messages becomes a wall of bright coral at night. Tweak: a deeper coral for own bubbles only (#A8472A, light text at 5.4:1), keeping the bright one for buttons and badges.

3. Sun yellow works as the Game Challenge signal. Keep it for that.

Because nothing else is yellow, a Challenge toast stands out anywhere in the app. Warnings should use a separate semantic colour, which the tokens ticket still has to define.

4. Coral does a lot of jobs on the chat screens

Own bubbles, the Send button, unread badges, links and the selected tab are all coral. It still reads clearly because bubbles are large and badges are small. If it gets noisy, unread badges are the first thing to move to ink.

5. What makes it Chaku is the shapes

Cream with coral is a common palette right now. What sets Chaku apart is Nunito's rounded letters, the bubble tail, pill controls and soft group avatars. Lean on those rather than adding more colour.

6. Check the phone tab bar in Ukrainian

"Сповіщення" is the longest label, and five tabs at 360px is tight. I used "Alerts" in the mockups, but voice-and-tone says Notification. Options: a shorter UK label, or an icon-only bell with a screen-reader label. Decide in the tokens ticket's Storybook check.
